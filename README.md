# POND HOP

This project is a small 2-player game about a duck and a frog who have to make their way through a swamp. I got the idea from my sister and her best friend, who are inseparable and call themselves 'Ducky' and 'Froggy'.

## How this was made
I used Unity 6 to develop Pond Hop.

## How to play
The frog, player 1, is controlled with A, D, and space bar. The duck, player 2, is controlled with the left, right, and up arrow keys.
It is hosted on itch.io and supports in-browser play, so no downloading is required! 
https://artemis9703.itch.io/pond-hop
